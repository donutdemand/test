🔑 Discord account tokens

Add as many account tokens as you like — single or bulk. Each one is checked against Discord and shown with its username. Full secrets never leave the server.

How to get an account token: open Discord in your browser → press Ctrl+Shift+I → Application tab → Local Storage → https://discord.com → copy the token value. Never share tokens with anyone — they give full access to the account. ⚠️ Automating user accounts violates Discord's ToS and can get accounts locked or banned; keep intervals generous.
